# Privacy Policy for Wallpaper Extension

This Privacy Policy describes how the Wallpaper Extension handles user data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **Local Storage:** All user configuration, notes, bookmarks, and boards are stored locally on your device's browser using `chrome.storage` and IndexedDB. We do not have access to this data.
- **Authentication Data:** If you choose to sign in using Google Account Sync, we access your basic profile information (such as email address) solely to authenticate and synchronize your settings across your devices.
- **API Requests:** Weather and currency widgets make direct calls to public API providers to fetch real-time updates. No personal identifying information is transmitted during these requests.

## 2. Data Sharing and Transfer
We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to any third parties. All data is processed strictly for the functionality of the extension.

## 3. Disclosures
- We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
- We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the item's single purpose.
- We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
